Guided transforms (D5, #40) in the local mode: Enter, F2 or a double-click on a Transform or Values cell opens the row's transform editor (copy, conversion with the input format, value map, quantity with a fixed UCUM unit or a unit column and its value map, coding with a display column; concatenation is not offered until the FML export can write it, #98) and the `repeat` of a multiple-choice row; "Add constant row" adds a row with a typed value and no column.
